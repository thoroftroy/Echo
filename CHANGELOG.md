# Echo changelog

## v0.1.4

- The editor now has a two-column landscape layout: waveform and controls on one side, editing panels on the other

## v0.1.3

- New setting: input boost for quiet microphones

## v0.1.2

- Speech to text with the time of every word, on the phone with no cloud. Pick words and delete them from the recording, or pick all the ums and uhs in one tap
- The speech model (about 40 MB, English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese or Italian) downloads once, with progress, when you first transcribe
- Search your recordings by name or by what was said

## v0.1.1

- Text size and accent colour chosen in Control Room now apply here
- New Rotate with the phone switch in Settings. Rotates by default

## v0.1.0

- First release. Record voice, instruments and music with a level meter, clipping warning, microphone choice and a metronome that is never recorded
- Studio Voice: one tap for clean, natural speech with Narration, Audiobook, Podcast, Dialogue, General and Music presets, adjustable intensity and a before and after switch
- Clean up tools for noise, hum, clicks, pops, harsh S sounds, breaths, room echo and long pauses
- Equalizer, noise gate, compressor, limiter, loudness targets, pitch, speed and voice changers such as Deeper, Bassy and Tenor
- Trim, delete, silence, fade, reverse, split at the playhead and add more audio to the end, all with unlimited undo and a way back to the original
- Open any audio file, and save as WAV, FLAC, M4A or OGG into Music/Echo where Cinema can use it as a voiceover
- Settings for recording defaults, count-in, metronome volume, waveform colour, Studio Voice starting preset and save format
- Split a recording at the playhead and add more audio to the end
