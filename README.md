# Echo

Record your voice, clean it up with one tap, and edit it, all on your phone and all offline.

- **Studio Voice.** One button for clean, natural narration: it removes background noise, hum, clicks and pops, and softens harsh S sounds and breaths.
- **Recording.** Level meter, clipping warning, microphone choice including USB microphones, mono or stereo, and a metronome that plays for you but is never recorded.
- **Editing.** Waveform with pinch zoom, trim, delete, split, join, fades, equalizer, compressor, limiter, loudness targets, pitch and speed. Every edit can be undone, and the original recording is always kept.
- **Voice changer.** Make a voice bassier, lighter or something else entirely.
- **Any audio.** Open any audio file, and save as WAV, FLAC, M4A or OGG. Saved files land in Music/Echo, ready for Cinema voiceovers.

No accounts, no ads, no cloud.
